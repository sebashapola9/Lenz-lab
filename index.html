How to use the right-hand rule

Point your right thumb along the induced magnetic field. Your curled fingers show the conventional current: out of the screen → counterclockwise; into the screen → clockwise. Electron drift is opposite to conventional current.

Suggested investigation

Keep the external field pointing into the screen. Compare increasing flux with decreasing flux. Next, reverse the field while keeping the same change. Finally, open the circuit. Explain why an emf can exist even when there is no sustained current.
